Evidence behind the course

We separate research findings from professional advice and from our own teaching recommendations. Each source below was opened and read on 4 November 2026.

Rules of thumb are not findings

We searched for experiments that set a slides-per-minute rate, a six-by-six word limit, or a minimum font size for projected slides, and found none. Published font advice ranges from 18 to 32 points, and legibility depends on screen size and viewing distance, so the best test is the back row of the actual room. The search was targeted, so this means we found no evidence and does not mean none exists.
