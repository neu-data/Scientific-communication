Scientific Communication

Writing, slide design and presentation skills for people who know their subject and want to communicate it clearly, without overstating their evidence.

Three-hour practical workshop

For 12 to 24 people. Exercises, feedback, timed rehearsal and a 12-minute break. Exactly 180 minutes.

One-hour talk

A realistic subset for any size of audience, with a brief activity and questions. Exactly 60 minutes.

Who it is for

  • Researchers preparing conference presentations.
  • Students preparing thesis defences.
  • Professionals in hospitals and other organisations presenting evidence, findings or recommendations.

The course is built for all disciplines, with health and research examples, and it welcomes people presenting in English as an additional language. Feedback looks at clarity and understanding, and never at accent or personality.

Learning outcomes

By the end of the workshop, participants should be able to:

  • Define their audience, purpose and main message.
  • Build an argument that connects a question, evidence, interpretation and implications.
  • Write clear scientific paragraphs, abstracts and slide headlines without overstating findings.
  • Design readable slides and explain figures accurately.
  • Deliver a timed presentation and respond to questions.
  • Use structured feedback to revise and rehearse their work.

This is an introduction with guided practice. It does not promise mastery of scientific writing or public speaking.

All case data in this course are fictional and labelled as such. CareCall and CoolRoom are teaching inventions, not real study results.
